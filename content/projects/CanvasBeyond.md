Developed a deep learning–based computer vision system for realistic image outpainting, extending images beyond their original boundaries with visually coherent results. Implemented a **completion network** as the core generator, supported by **global** and **local context discriminators** during training to ensure both large-scale realism and fine-detail accuracy. Leveraged the **Places365** dataset, focusing on 3,500 curated beach images, and trained over 25 epochs for scene-specific fidelity. Designed the architecture using **Keras** with multiple convolutional layers, enabling generation of images up to **256×256** pixels. Integrated **OpenCV** for dynamic image preprocessing and resizing, ensuring input compatibility. Achieved high-quality outputs by combining adversarial training with discriminator-guided refinements, demonstrating strong performance in generative image extension tasks.
