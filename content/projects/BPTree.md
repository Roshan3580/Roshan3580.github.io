Implemented a high-performance **B+Tree** data structure for efficient storage, retrieval, and management of large, sorted datasets. Supported dynamic **insertions**, **deletions**, and **range queries** with logarithmic time complexity, ensuring scalability for expanding datasets. Added features for exporting the current tree state for grading or visualization, and generating **graphical representations** of the B+Tree structure. Designed with keys stored in an unordered heap to maintain operational efficiency, making the implementation suitable for **database indexing**, **file systems**, and educational demonstrations of balanced search trees.
